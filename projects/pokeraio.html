THE WRITE-UP

What it is

A poker platform for playing with friends and bots, without paying just to play among yourselves. It has multiplayer tables and bot play, hand histories, friends and clubs, rankings and rewards, and AI analysis of the hands you play or import.

Under the hood

The game logic is the core of it: turn order and hand state have to keep making sense as people act. The social features and the platform around it are built on top of that.

It also includes shuffle-verification features.
