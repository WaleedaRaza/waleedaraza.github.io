THE WRITE-UP

What it does

Libxndr is a library and a sourcing tool in one. Search a title or an author and it searches the open web, checks whether a result behaves like a real PDF, estimates its length, and ranks the strongest leads. It points you to the original source; you decide what belongs on your shelf.

The finder works without an account. An account lets you run a whole reading list at once and keep a private shelf.

The shared library

Anyone can contribute a book: a public link, their own PDF, or a result from the finder. Every contribution starts on your private shelf, and a review decides whether it joins the public vault.

What's next

Ask a question and get sent to the specific section of a book that answers it, using a parsed structure of the text. The aim is to get you to the source and the passage around it, not to replace the reading.
