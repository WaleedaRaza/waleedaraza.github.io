LOCK A POST

Paste an essay and a password. You get an entry for blawg/posts.js that holds only scrambled text. Readers on the site type the password to read it.

If the text has no HTML tags, blank lines become <p> paragraphs and lines starting with "## " become headings.

This is the only protection. Use a long passphrase (four or more random words). If you lose it, the post can't be recovered from the site.

Checked: it decrypts back to your text with this password. Keep your plain essay somewhere outside the repo (or in a private note).
