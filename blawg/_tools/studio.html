BLAWG STUDIO

Seal essays and hand out the codes that open them. Everything happens on this computer. The site only ever gets scrambled text.

1 · YOUR MASTER PASSPHRASE

One passphrase for the whole blog. Every essay's key comes from it, so nothing else needs to be stored. It is never saved and never goes in the repo. Use five or more random words and keep it in a password manager: if it is lost, every essay has to be sealed again and every code remade.

Connecting the folder (Chrome or Edge) lets the studio write its files straight into blawg/sealed/. Without it, files are downloaded and you move them there yourself.

2 · SEAL AN ESSAY

The list is the board in blawg/posts.js. Sealing an essay again replaces it, and codes already handed out keep working.

If the text has no HTML tags, blank lines become paragraphs, lines starting with "## " become headings and lines starting with "> " become quotes.

4 · CODES YOU'VE HANDED OUT

Revoking stops a code from opening anything from then on. It can't take back what someone already read.

5 · THE BOARD

Everything readers see in the list. Drag a piece from the left onto an essay's art slot (or click a piece, then click a slot). Hover a piece to see it. Rename titles in place, tick picks and N, write the public preview, add new titles at the top. Then press SAVE BOARD: it writes blawg/posts.js.
